# r5e Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0 — 5 September 2026

This agreement is between **r5e Inc.** (the **Company**) and the individual or legal entity identified in the signature record (**Contributor**). It becomes effective when both parties accept it in the signature record.

## 1. Scope

The covered projects are **r5e-kernel** and **r5e-enterprise**, maintained by the Company at https://gitlab.com/r5e-group/r5e-kernel and https://gitlab.com/r5e-group/r5e-enterprise, including subsequent locations of those same projects. This agreement does not cover r5e-core or automatically extend to other projects.

A **Contribution** is code, documentation, tests, artwork, or other material that Contributor deliberately submits for incorporation into a covered project after this agreement takes effect. Earlier submissions are covered only if identified in the signature record. Merely discussing an idea, reporting a problem, or maintaining an independent fork does not submit that material under this agreement. Material clearly identified as excluded from this agreement is not a Contribution.

Contributor keeps ownership of its Contributions and may continue using and licensing them independently.

For the duration of the applicable rights, Contributor authorizes the Company, worldwide and without royalties, to copy, modify, translate, publish, distribute, perform, display, and otherwise exploit Contributions and works incorporating them, in source or compiled form. This permission is nonexclusive and irrevocable, and includes authority to grant sublicenses through successive recipients.

**The Company may offer Contributions and works incorporating them under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3 (the covered projects' license, SPDX `AGPL-3.0-only`), other open-source licenses, or commercial and proprietary terms, without further permission or payment to Contributor.** This agreement does not require future Company versions to remain open source. It does not revoke licenses already granted to recipients of an open-source version, or give the Company rights in material that Contributor does not control.

## 3. Patent permission

Contributor also authorizes the Company and its sublicensees to practice patent claims that Contributor can license and that are necessarily practiced by a Contribution, either by itself or together with the project as it stood when submitted. This worldwide, nonexclusive, royalty-free permission covers making, commissioning manufacture, using, importing, selling, and supplying such material, and includes successive sublicensing. It lasts for the applicable patent term and is irrevocable except as follows.

A recipient loses the patent permission granted to it under this agreement for a covered project if it initiates a patent claim in court, including a counterclaim, alleging infringement by that project or a Contribution within it. Termination takes effect upon filing and does not terminate permissions held by other recipients that have not brought such a claim.

## 4. Authority and provenance

Contributor confirms that it has the authority to grant these permissions. An individual whose employer or client owns relevant rights must obtain authority covering this agreement, including commercial relicensing; permission merely to publish code is insufficient. A person signing for an entity confirms authority to bind that entity. Affiliates are not included merely by association.

Contributor must identify third-party material, its source, and known license restrictions when submitting it. Such identification does not relicense that material or represent that Contributor owns it. Contributor must also disclose known restrictions that conflict with this agreement and promptly notify the Company if it learns that an earlier authority statement was incorrect.

The same requirements apply to submissions prepared with AI assistance. Using a tool does not expand the rights Contributor can grant. This agreement grants only rights that exist and that Contributor is entitled to license.

## 5. Other rights and responsibilities

Where legally permitted, Contributor consents to editing, combining, and publishing Contributions without individual approval, and agrees not to assert moral rights to prevent uses authorized here. This does not authorize false attribution or endorsement. No trademark license is granted except permission to identify Contributor accurately in customary attribution.

Contributor promises no maintenance or support. Apart from the express authority statements above, Contributions carry no warranty, including any implied warranty of merchantability, fitness, or noninfringement, to the extent the law permits. This agreement creates no obligation for Contributor to indemnify the Company and no employment, agency, or partnership relationship. The Company need not accept or use a submission.

## 6. Continuity and changes

Contributor may stop submitting Contributions at any time; permissions for existing Contributions continue. The Company may transfer this agreement to a successor that assumes its obligations in connection with a transfer of a covered project or the associated business. Changing maintainers alone does not change the legal recipient.

Amendments require both parties' express agreement. Replacing a document on a website does not amend a signed agreement. If a provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues to the extent legally possible. This agreement does not override mandatory applicable law or third-party license obligations.

## 7. Signature record

By signing, each party agrees to this version of the agreement. An electronic signature must identify this agreement's version and exact retained text (its SHA-256). The signing individual affirms an intention to sign; an account identifier alone is not a signature.

**Public part** — one line in the covered project's `CLA-SIGNERS.md`, added by merge request: the Contributor's contribution account identifier(s) (hosting service and account), the agreement version, the SHA-256 of this text, the date, and the countersigning Company representative's account.
