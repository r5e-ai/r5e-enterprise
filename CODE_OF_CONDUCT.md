# Code of conduct

The charter in r5e-core says what this project is for. Three of its lines
apply to the people in it as much as to the code:

- Nothing hidden. Say what you did and why, in the open, under your name.
- Anyone can verify. A claim is checkable or it is an opinion; say which.
- Your key is yours. Do not speak for someone else's account, work or key.

On top of those: no harassment, no personal attacks, no publishing another
person's private information. Disagree with the argument.

## Reporting

Open a confidential issue on the canonical project (see `SECURITY.md` for
how), or write to a maintainer listed in `MAINTAINERS`.

## Who decides

The steward. A report is answered within 5 working days with what was found
and what was done. The outcomes are a private warning, a temporary block, or
removal from the project's spaces. The steward's own conduct is reviewed by
the same rule, in public.
