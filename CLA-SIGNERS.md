# Contributors who have agreed to CLA.md

One line per signature, added by merge request. Fields: account (hosting service and account
identifier) · agreement version · `cla:sha256:` of the exact CLA.md text agreed to (recompute
with `shasum -a 256 CLA.md`) · date · countersigned-by (a maintainer's account). The completed
signature form, with names and signatures, is retained privately by r5e Inc.; nothing personal
appears here. A new CLA version requires a new line.
